# NyumbaNest User Manual

## 1. Introduction

NyumbaNest helps people find homes, rentals, land, and commercial properties in Tanzania. Buyers and tenants can browse listings, search by location and features, contact property owners, and save properties. Property owners can create and manage their own listings.

## 2. Getting started

1. Open the NyumbaNest web address provided by your organization.
2. To browse, choose **Properties** in the main navigation, then select **All Properties**, **Buy**, **Rent**, **Land**, or **Commercial**. You can also search from the home page.
3. To create an account, choose the profile icon. If you are signed out, you will be directed to **Log in**, where you can choose **Create an account**.
4. Enter your full name, email address, and password, then choose **Create account**. Your password must be at least 8 characters and include a letter and a number.
5. Check your email and verify your account before creating or managing listings.

## 3. Email verification and login

### Verify your email

After registration, NyumbaNest opens **Verify your email** and asks you to check your inbox. Enter the email address used to register and the verification code from your email, then choose **Verify email**. You can also verify using the link in the email. If needed, choose **Resend verification email** after the countdown ends.

### Sign in and sign out

1. Choose the profile icon, enter your email address and password on **Log in**, and select **Sign in**.
2. To sign out, open **My Profile** from the profile icon or mobile **Profile** navigation, then choose **Sign Out**.
3. To leave a sign-in, registration, or recovery page, choose the **X** button in the upper-right corner or press **Escape**. This returns to the previous page when available.

### Recover a forgotten password

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address and choose **Send reset link**.
3. Use the link in the email to open the password reset page, or enter the recovery code in the form shown on the **Forgot password** page and choose **Verify recovery code**.
4. Enter and confirm a new password, then choose **Update password**. Use at least 8 characters, including a letter and a number.

## 4. Using the dashboard

- On desktop, the main navigation includes **Home**, **Properties**, **My Listings**, and **Pricing**. Open **Properties** to choose **All Properties**, **Buy**, **Rent**, **Land**, or **Commercial**.
- On a phone, open the menu for the same main navigation and property categories. The bottom navigation provides **Home**, **Search**, **Saved**, **Listings**, and **Profile**.
- The home page includes a moving photo strip and a quick search for location, property type, and whether you want to buy or rent. The photos pause on hover or keyboard focus; reduced-motion settings show them without animation. Choose **Search** to view results.
- On **Search** or **Browse**, you can filter by location, transaction, property type, minimum and maximum price, minimum bedrooms, and minimum area. Choose a sort order and select **Apply filters**.
- Select a property card to view its details, photos, features, and inquiry form. Published listings may show a **Verified** badge.
- To contact an owner, enter **Your name**, **Email address**, and a **Message** on the property page, then choose **Send inquiry**. A phone number is optional, and you do not need to sign in. Your contact details are shared with the owner for that inquiry.

## 5. Managing properties

Property management requires an account with a verified email address.

### Create a listing

1. Choose **List a Property** or **My Listings**, then select **Add Property** or **Upload Your First Property**.
2. Enter the property details and location. Add photos if you wish: up to 10 JPEG, PNG, WebP, or AVIF images, with a maximum size of 10 MiB each.
3. Choose **Save draft** to keep the listing as a draft, or **Submit for review** to send it for moderation.

New listings are **not published automatically**. Submitting for review does not publish a property; it remains unpublished until moderation is complete.

### View, edit, and delete listings

- In **My Listings**, view your listing cards, their statuses, and listing counts. Use **Filter listings** to show a particular status.
- Choose **Preview** to review a listing or **Edit** to change its details and photos. Choose **Add photos** to upload images; use the remove buttons on photos you want to take off the listing.
- Saving changes to a published, rented, or sold listing sends it back for review. Owners cannot publish or verify listings themselves.
- To delete a listing, choose **Delete**, then confirm with **Delete property**. This permanently removes the listing and its uploaded photos.

## 6. Saved properties and profile

- To save a published property, choose its heart button. Sign in if prompted.
- Open **Saved** in the mobile bottom navigation to view saved properties. Choose the filled heart on a saved card to remove it.
- Choose the profile icon to open **My Profile**. The page shows your account name and email, lets you upload a JPEG, PNG, or WebP profile photo (maximum 5 MiB), and links to **My Listings**, **Saved Properties**, and **Subscription & Billing**. **Subscription & Billing** opens the pricing information page; subscription plans and payments are not currently available. Choose **Sign Out** to end your session.

## 7. Administrator property moderation

Administrators can open **Admin** in the main menu to view listings from all users. Each listing can be edited or previewed, and administrators can delete the listing and its photos. The **Approve** action is available for listings awaiting review; approving publishes the listing on the public marketplace. Editing a published listing sends it back for review. Approval does not automatically apply the separate **Verified** badge.

Administrator access is assigned by a trusted NyumbaNest operator through Supabase. Entering “Admin” as the account's full name during signup does not grant administrator access.

## 8. Troubleshooting

- **Verification email has not arrived:** Check your spam or junk folder and confirm that you entered the correct email address. On **Verify your email**, use **Resend verification email** when the countdown ends.
- **The verification code or link is rejected:** It may have expired or already been used. Request a fresh code and try again.
- **Sign-in fails:** Check your email address and password. If you have not verified your email, return to **Verify your email**. If you forgot the password, use **Forgot password?**
- **A form will not submit:** Complete required fields, correct any highlighted errors, and follow the limits shown. For property photos, check the supported formats and 10 MiB per-image limit.
- **A listing is not visible publicly:** Drafts and listings awaiting moderation are unpublished. After submitting a listing, wait for moderation to finish.
- **Listings or saved properties do not load:** Choose **Retry** when shown. If the problem continues, try again later.

## 9. Support

The application does not currently provide a verified support email address, phone number, or contact link.
